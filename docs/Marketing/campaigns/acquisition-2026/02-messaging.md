# Audiences and messaging

## Message hierarchy

| Level | Message |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign platform | **Tu talento crea. Solennix organiza.** |
| Primary promise | Todo tu negocio de eventos, en un solo lugar |
| Functional proof | Eventos, clientes, cotizaciones, contratos, inventario, personal, checklists y control de pagos |
| Emotional benefit | Más control detrás de escena; más confianza frente al cliente |
| Offer | Empieza gratis, sin tarjeta |
| Activation CTA | Crea tu primer evento gratis |
| Brand signature | Cada detalle importa |

## Audience matrix

| Segment | Trigger situation | Pain | Desired progress | Demonstration | Best hook |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wedding/social planner | Several events overlap | Details buried in chats and notes | See every event and next action clearly | Event dashboard + checklist + client | “Tu evento no debería vivir en 7 lugares.” |
| Catering/banquets | Quotes, ingredients, stock, and deposits move together | Manual recalculation and stock uncertainty | Quote and prepare with operational visibility | Products/recipes + inventory + quote | “Cotizar no debería obligarte a rehacer todo.” |
| Decorator/florist | Reusable items and consumables rotate across dates | Double-booked items and fragmented client references | Know what is available and for which event | Inventory + calendar + event detail | “Que una pieza esté en 2 eventos no debería descubrirse tarde.” |
| Venue | Leads, dates, packages, and staff coordination | Slow response and inconsistent follow-up | Respond professionally and protect availability | Public profile + requests + calendar | “De la solicitud al evento, sin perder el contexto.” |
| DJ/audio/lighting | Packages, equipment, crew, and payments | Information changes after the quote | Keep package, equipment, team, and balance together | Products + equipment + staff + payment tracking | “Tu equipo técnico merece un sistema, no otra nota.” |
| Multi-service team | Owners delegate execution | Everyone has a different version of the plan | Shared operational truth | Staff + teams + event detail + portal | “Un solo evento. Una sola versión de la verdad.” |

## Objection handling

| Objection | Response | Proof asset |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Ya uso Excel y WhatsApp.” | Keep the tools for conversation; move operational truth into one system designed for events. | Before/after workflow carousel |
| “No tengo tiempo para aprender otra app.” | Start with one real event and add only the information you need today. | 10-minute first-event demo |
| “Mi negocio es pequeño.” | Small teams suffer most when one person must remember everything. Start free and grow the system with the business. | Solo-organizer story |
| “Trabajo desde el celular.” | Solennix is available on iOS, Android, and web. | Multi-platform visual |
| “No quiero meter los pagos en otra plataforma.” | Solennix does not move the client's money; it records external/manual payments and outstanding balances for operational control. | Payment-tracking explainer |
| “Mis eventos son diferentes.” | Configura productos, servicios, inventario, personal y detalles según la operación real del negocio. | Demo específico para el segmento |

## Approved claims

These claims are supported by current product sources:

- “Disponible en web, iOS y Android.”
- “Gestiona eventos, clientes, productos, inventario, personal y checklists.”
- “Crea cotizaciones y contratos profesionales.”
- “Registra pagos externos/manuales y saldos pendientes.”
- “Empieza gratis, sin tarjeta.”
- “Interfaz en español e inglés y soporte de múltiples monedas.”
- “Comparte un portal del cliente y enlaces de solicitud según las capacidades de cada plataforma.”

## Claims requiring evidence before use

- Number of active users, downloads, businesses, or events managed
- Time saved, revenue increased, conversion uplift, or reduced errors
- Ratings, review counts, customer quotes, or star graphics
- “Best,” “number one,” “most complete,” or category leadership
- Any plan limit, price, or feature entitlement not rechecked against current monetization sources
- Any privacy/security wording beyond published policies and verified implementation

## Copy voice

- Use clear Mexican/neutral LATAM Spanish in campaign artifacts.
- Sound warm, capable, and composed—not jokey, desperate, or corporate.
- Speak to the organizer as a skilled professional, never as someone who “cannot organize.”
- Keep the product name as **Solennix**, not all caps.
- Prefer observable actions: “crea,” “consulta,” “registra,” “comparte,” “organiza.”
