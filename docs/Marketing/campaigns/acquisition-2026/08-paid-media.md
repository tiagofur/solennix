# Paid media pilot

## Decision

Paid media is a **validation system**, not the first launch step. Start organic production immediately, but do not activate paid spend until campaign source can be connected to registration and first-event activation.

## Pilot scope

| Item | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Market | Mexico |
| Duration | 30 days after measurement gate |
| Objective | Activated organizers, not subscriptions |
| Primary destination | Web registration with platform-specific UTMs |
| Secondary destination | Store-specific campaigns only after install-to-activation tracking is verified |
| Minimum useful creative set | 6 vertical videos, 3 portrait statics/carousels, 3 segment adaptations |

## Recommended budget envelope

No spend is authorized by this document. After explicit approval, use percentages rather than assuming identical economics across networks.

| Channel | Initial share | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Meta | 50% | Broad prospecting, multi-placement delivery, retargeting |
| TikTok | 25% | Native short-video discovery and creator-style testing |
| YouTube | 15% | Shorts discovery plus tutorial/video remarketing |
| Experiment reserve | 10% | Pinterest, LinkedIn, creator boost, or winning-channel expansion |

If the approved budget cannot give each active test enough conversions to learn, reduce the number of channels—do not split tiny amounts everywhere.

## Campaign structure

### Meta

- Prospecting campaign: broad Mexico + language, with segment creatives rather than over-narrow interests.
- Retargeting campaign: engaged site visitors/register viewers who have not registered, subject to consent and audience-size policy.
- Creative cells: campaign platform × pain diagnostic × first-event proof.
- Use placement-specific 9:16 Reels/Stories assets with audio and safe-zone compliance.

### TikTok

- Use native 9:16, sound-on, people-led creative.
- Begin with organic videos that generated qualified profile visits, comments, saves, or register clicks.
- Test Spark Ads only with appropriate account access and creator authorization.
- Rotate fresh hooks before changing the entire offer.

### YouTube

- Shorts: 10–30s action-focused cuts.
- Tutorial remarketing: users who watched demos but did not register.
- Keep multiple vertical messages in the ad group; do not upload only landscape assets.

## Test matrix

| Test | Variable A | Variable B | Constant | Primary read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 Hook | “7 lugares” | “No migres todo” | Audience, proof, CTA | Qualified register views |
| T2 Segment | Planners | Catering | Platform, format, CTA | Activated users by segment |
| T3 Proof | Screen demo | Talking head + screen | Hook, audience, CTA | Registration completion |
| T4 CTA | Crea tu primer evento | Empieza gratis | Creative, audience | Activation within 7 days |
| T5 Destination | Campaign landing | Direct register | Creative, audience | Activated-user rate |

Change one meaningful variable per controlled test. Platform delivery systems may require consolidation; do not fragment the account into dozens of low-volume ad sets.

## Decision rules

### Stop

- Tracking is broken or cannot reconcile source → registered → activated.
- Creative produces clicks but no qualified registrations after the minimum observation window.
- High negative-feedback, policy, brand-safety, or consent signal.
- Campaign claims or destination do not match the product.

### Iterate

- Hook generates qualified visits but register-page completion is weak.
- Registrations occur but activation is weak: fix onboarding/first-event handoff before buying more traffic.
- One segment responds but the proof is unclear: build segment-specific demonstrations.

### Scale

- Activated-user volume increases while cost per activated user stays inside the approved guardrail.
- Activation quality and 7/30-day retention do not deteriorate.
- At least 2 creative variants work, reducing dependence on one asset.

## Reporting

Daily: spend, delivery, tracking health, registrations, obvious safety issues.  
Twice weekly: creative and segment diagnosis.  
Weekly: activated users, CPAU, activation rate, quality/retention proxy, decision and next test.

Never scale from CPM, reach, video views, CTR, or raw registrations alone.
