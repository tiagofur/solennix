# Channel execution plan

## Shared production rule

Create every campaign idea as a **content packet**:

1. One 9:16 video master with captions and sound.
2. One 4:5 feed visual or 6–8-slide carousel.
3. One 1:1 fallback.
4. One short post, one long post, and one community-first version.
5. One tracked destination URL.

Adapt pacing, hook, framing, caption, and CTA per channel. Do not auto-post identical copy everywhere.

## Instagram

| Surface | Cadence | Job | Format |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Reels | 4/week | Reach and product proof | 9:16, 10–30s, sound-on, captions, hook in first 2s |
| Carousels | 2/week | Saves and consideration | 4:5, 6–8 slides, one idea per slide |
| Stories | Daily | Conversation and conversion | Polls, questions, demos, link sticker |
| Live | 2/month | Trust and onboarding | “Organiza conmigo” first-event session |

Organic CTA: **“Crea tu primer evento gratis — link en bio.”**

Paid role: broad prospecting and retargeting. Use placement-specific 9:16 assets with audio and keep key elements in safe zones.

## Facebook

| Surface | Cadence | Job | Format |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Reels | 3/week | Incremental reach | Recut Instagram winners with Facebook-native caption |
| Feed | 3/week | Education and demonstrations | 4:5 image/video, longer explanatory copy |
| Groups | 5 meaningful interactions/week | Trust and research | Answer-first posts; links only when useful and allowed |
| Live | 2/month | Community workshop | Event setup, quote workflow, Q&A |

Group rule: never lead with “download my app.” Lead with a useful checklist, answer, or operational framework. Disclose affiliation when mentioning Solennix.

## TikTok

| Content type | Cadence | Pattern |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Pain recognition | 2/week | Native talking-head or hands-on desk POV |
| Screen demo | 2/week | Fast workflow, zoomed UI, voiceover |
| Organizer education | 1/week | One operational tip with a concrete example |
| Founder/build-in-public | 1/week | Why a workflow exists; what users taught us |

Production: 9:16, at least 720p, sound-on, UI-safe captions, people when possible. Paid tests should begin with organic posts that already show qualified intent.

## YouTube

| Surface | Cadence | Job |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Shorts | 3/week | Discovery and proof |
| Tutorials | 1/week | Search, activation, support |
| Playlists | Ongoing | Segment and job-based discovery |

Shorts: 9:16, 10–30 seconds for action-focused ads, fast branding, explicit direction. Tutorials: 4–8 minutes, show the result first, then the steps.

Initial playlists:

- Empieza con Solennix
- Cotizaciones y contratos
- Inventario para eventos
- Operación de bodas y eventos sociales
- Catering y banquetes
- Equipo y personal

## LinkedIn

Cadence: 3 posts/week from the company page and founder profile.

Use for professionalization, operational lessons, event-industry insights, partnerships, and product-building evidence. Avoid turning it into a duplicate product feed.

Recommended formats:

- 4:5 subtitled video
- Document carousel: “The event operations checklist”
- Founder post: lesson learned from event businesses
- Case study only after verifiable customer evidence exists

## Pinterest

Cadence: 5 Pins/week, scheduled in batches.

Use search-led evergreen assets:

- Checklists for weddings, quinceañeras, catering, and corporate events
- Quote and inventory templates
- Event timeline diagrams
- Before/after workflow visuals
- Tutorial Pins linking to a relevant landing or video

Primary format: 2:3, 1000×1500. Use keyword-rich titles and descriptions; do not rely on hashtags alone.

## WhatsApp Business

Use only with consent and reasonable frequency.

| Audience | Cadence | Content |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Opted-in prospects | Maximum 1 useful message/week | Checklist, workshop, feature demo, first-event guide |
| Personal referrals | One-to-one | Short context, benefit, tracked link |
| Existing users | Separate lifecycle | Activation education, not acquisition ads |

Never add people to broadcast lists without consent. Do not pressure contacts or imply limited availability.

## Threads and X

Cadence: 1–2 posts/day, primarily repurposed insights and conversation.

- Short operational observations
- Polls about workflows
- Build-in-public product decisions
- Threads that expand a checklist or teardown
- Replies to event-industry conversations

X paid media remains experimental until organic conversations show qualified demand.

## Profiles and conversion hygiene

Every profile must have:

- Consistent Solennix logo and campaign bio
- One clear link with platform-specific UTMs
- Pinned launch manifesto
- Pinned 15-second product demonstration
- Pinned first-event onboarding guide
- Accurate web, App Store, and Google Play availability
- No stale “coming soon” copy
