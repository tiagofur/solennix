# Acquisition strategy

## Outcome first

Build a repeatable acquisition engine that turns event professionals who currently coordinate work across WhatsApp, spreadsheets, notes, calendars, and memory into activated Solennix organizers.

The campaign optimizes the path:

`qualified reach → landing visit → register view → registered → verified → first login → first event created within 7 days`

Subscription behavior is not part of this campaign's scorecard.

## Core insight

Event professionals are not disorganized people. They are running complex operations with tools that were never designed to work together. The campaign must respect their expertise while making the system problem visible.

## Campaign platform

### Central idea

**Tu talento crea. Solennix organiza.**

### Promise

Put events, clients, quotes, inventory, payment tracking, staff, and operational details in one professional system so the organizer can focus on delivering memorable events.

### Tension

The work looks magical to the client, but behind the scenes critical details are scattered across chats, files, notes, and separate apps.

### Proof

- Real app workflows and verified screenshots
- A complete event visible in one place
- Quotes and contracts generated from operational data
- Client, inventory, staff, checklist, and payment-tracking views
- Web, iOS, and Android availability

### CTA

**Crea tu primer evento gratis.**

This is more concrete than “learn more” and connects acquisition to activation.

## Funnel design

| Stage | User question | Campaign job | Primary content | Conversion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | “Why does every event feel chaotic?” | Name the system problem without blaming the organizer | Pain-point Reels, memes with dignity, diagnostics | Qualified video view or engaged visit |
| Consideration | “Can one tool really replace my current mess?” | Demonstrate one workflow at a time | Screen demos, carousels, comparisons, tutorials | Register-page view |
| Conversion | “Will setup be worth it?” | Make the first step feel small and safe | First-event walkthrough, no-card offer, platform proof | Registration |
| Activation | “What should I do first?” | Guide the organizer to a useful first event | 10-minute setup checklist, onboarding messages | First event within 7 days |
| Referral | “Who else needs this?” | Turn useful wins into trusted introductions | Shareable checklist and referral prompt | Referred activated organizer |

## Audience sequencing

1. **Primary:** independent wedding and social-event planners.
2. **Secondary:** catering/banquet businesses and decorators/florists.
3. **Expansion:** venues, rental suppliers, DJs, audio/lighting providers, and multi-service teams.

Do not mix all segments in one ad. Keep the platform constant and change the pain, demonstration, and proof.

## Channel priorities

| Tier | Channels | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core | Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | Reach, identification, product demonstration, remarketing |
| Support | Facebook Groups, WhatsApp Business, YouTube tutorials, Pinterest | Trust, search/discovery, education, community referrals |
| Professional | LinkedIn | Founder credibility, operations content, partnerships |
| Experimental | Threads and X | Conversation, build-in-public, repurposed insights |

Being present everywhere does **not** mean equal investment everywhere. Produce a shared creative idea, then adapt it to how each channel is consumed.

## 90-day operating phases

| Phase | Days | Goal | Exit criterion |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Foundation | 0–7 | Attribution, profiles, links, baseline, production system | P0 measurement verified |
| Organic proof | 1–14 | Identify messages and formats that attract qualified organizers | At least 3 creatives with strong downstream intent signal |
| Paid validation | 15–30 | Test segment × message × creative combinations | At least 1 repeatable activated-user path |
| Scale winners | 31–60 | Increase output and spend only on quality growth | CPAU stable within approved guardrail |
| Compounding | 61–90 | Add creators, partnerships, SEO video, referrals | Multiple acquisition sources and declining dependency on one channel |

## Non-negotiables

- No fake urgency, fake testimonials, fabricated user counts, or unsupported performance claims.
- No “collect payments in Solennix” wording. Say **record and track external/manual payments**.
- Do not advertise unshipped or platform-incomplete features as universally available.
- Every asset has one CTA and one measurable destination.
- Paid media is a controlled experiment, not a substitute for measurement or product activation.
