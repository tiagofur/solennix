# Launch runbook

## Owners

Assign one accountable owner per role even when one person holds several roles.

| Role | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign lead | Objective, calendar, decisions, launch readiness |
| Product truth owner | Feature and claim verification |
| Creative lead | Production system, quality, variants |
| Community lead | Groups, replies, partnerships, consent |
| Analytics owner | Attribution, dashboard, data QA |
| Paid media owner | Account structure, budget, experiments, safety |

## Day 0 — Freeze the campaign truth

- [ ] Confirm `solennix.com/register`, App Store, and Google Play destinations.
- [ ] Re-check current features and platform parity.
- [ ] Approve the claim list in `02-messaging.md`.
- [ ] Remove stale “coming soon” copy from every social profile.
- [ ] Confirm no asset contains real client PII.
- [ ] Confirm who can approve public posts and spend.

## Day 1 — Measurement

- [ ] Complete `09-measurement.md` P0 or document the paid-media block.
- [ ] Create the link registry and test every URL.
- [ ] Record the organic baseline.
- [ ] Exclude internal traffic.
- [ ] Verify registration methods and first-login counts.

## Day 2 — Profile reset

- [ ] Update bios and links.
- [ ] Upload consistent profile/cover assets.
- [ ] Pin the manifesto, 15-second product demo, and first-event guide.
- [ ] Set up highlights: Empieza, Eventos, Cotiza, Inventario, Clientes, Equipo.
- [ ] Create YouTube playlists and Pinterest boards.

## Day 3 — Production batch 1

- [ ] Render static master assets.
- [ ] Record V01, V02, and V03.
- [ ] Export captioned and clean versions.
- [ ] Create alt text, transcripts, and thumbnails.
- [ ] Run brand, claim, accessibility, and link QA.

## Days 4–7 — Organic launch

- [ ] Publish manifesto.
- [ ] Publish chaos diagnostic.
- [ ] Publish first-event walkthrough.
- [ ] Contribute the operational checklist to relevant communities.
- [ ] Reply to every substantive question within one business day.
- [ ] Log objections using the audience/message taxonomy.

## Week 2 — Validate intent

- [ ] Publish quote/client workflows and the first live onboarding.
- [ ] Compare register intent by hook and format.
- [ ] Interview at least 5 target professionals; do not ask them for praise.
- [ ] Produce responses to the top 3 objections.
- [ ] Choose paid-test candidates only from evidence, not personal preference.

## Weeks 3–4 — Segment proof

- [ ] Run catering and decorator content arcs.
- [ ] Begin creator outreach with clear disclosure and usage rights.
- [ ] Launch paid pilot only if measurement gate is green and spend is approved.
- [ ] Review source → activation twice weekly.

## Weeks 5–6 — Expand and decide

- [ ] Run DJ/audio, venue, staff, and payment-tracking content.
- [ ] Publish a transparent learning report using verified data.
- [ ] Stop weak source/creative combinations.
- [ ] Build the next 6-week calendar from observed activation, not vanity metrics.

## Daily operating checklist

- [ ] Posts scheduled and links tested
- [ ] Comments, DMs, and group replies handled
- [ ] Tracking health checked
- [ ] Claim/policy issues escalated
- [ ] Audience objections logged
- [ ] Creative fatigue signal noted

## Weekly review agenda · 45 minutes

1. Tracking health — 5 min
2. Activated users and CPAU — 10 min
3. Funnel drop-offs — 10 min
4. Creative/segment evidence — 10 min
5. Stop, iterate, scale decisions — 5 min
6. Owners and next production batch — 5 min

## Incident rules

Pause affected distribution immediately when:

- A claim is inaccurate or feature availability changed.
- A link or registration flow is broken.
- PII appears in an asset.
- Rights/consent for music, footage, or talent are unclear.
- Tracking double-counts or cannot reconcile conversions.
- A platform flags policy or account-security issues.

Document the incident, affected asset IDs, correction, republish decision, and data exclusion window.
