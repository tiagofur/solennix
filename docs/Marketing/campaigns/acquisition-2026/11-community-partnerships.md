# Community, creators, and partnerships

## Principle

Trust cannot be automated. Community acquisition should make event professionals more capable before asking them to register.

## Community map

Prioritize communities where target users already exchange operational knowledge:

- Wedding and event-planning associations
- Facebook groups for wedding planners, catering, banquet services, decorators, venues, DJs, and rental suppliers
- Local chamber and entrepreneur communities
- Event-industry schools and certification programs
- WhatsApp communities with explicit admin permission
- YouTube educators and TikTok/Instagram creators focused on event operations

Maintain [templates/partner-pipeline.csv](templates/partner-pipeline.csv) with owner, status, permission, audience fit, and next action.

## Contribution ladder

1. Listen and log recurring operational questions.
2. Answer questions without linking to Solennix.
3. Share a useful checklist or template with affiliation disclosure.
4. Offer a free workshop or teardown.
5. Invite interested participants to test one real workflow.
6. Request a case study only after real use and written approval.

## Creator selection

Score 1–5 on:

- Audience is made of working event professionals
- Comments show real operational questions
- Creator demonstrates processes, not only aesthetics
- Tone matches the Solennix brand
- Geography/language matches the campaign
- Past sponsorships are disclosed clearly
- Content quality is consistent without inflated claims

Follower count is secondary to audience fit and trust.

## Collaboration formats

| Format | Deliverable | Evidence rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow review | Creator tries one real event flow | Must distinguish demo from endorsement |
| Educational collab | Joint checklist or tutorial | Solennix is the enabling tool, not the whole lesson |
| Live clinic | Organizer workflow Q&A | No public client PII |
| Build feedback | Creator critiques a workflow | Honest limitations are allowed |
| Partner ad | Boost approved creator content | Written ad-usage authorization required |

## Rights and consent record

Before publication or boosting, record:

- Person and account
- Asset IDs
- Organic usage period and channels
- Paid usage period and geography
- Editing/cropping rights
- Whitelisting/partnership-ad permission
- Compensation
- Disclosure language
- Music/footage ownership
- Revocation and takedown contact

## Interview-to-proof workflow

1. Interview to learn, not to collect praise.
2. Ask permission to quote only after the interview.
3. Draft the exact quote and context.
4. Send it for written approval.
5. Keep source evidence and approved usage scope.
6. Never add performance numbers the participant did not verify.

## Referral loop

After an organizer creates a first event:

1. Deliver a useful “first event completed” checklist.
2. Ask one qualitative question: “¿Qué parte te dio más claridad?”
3. If the response is positive, invite them to share a neutral referral link.
4. Do not attach plan discounts or incentives until separately approved.

## Community KPIs

- Qualified conversations
- Checklist/template saves or downloads
- Workshop attendance
- Register views from community links
- Activated referred organizers
- Activation rate by partner/community
- Complaints, removals, or admin warnings

Stop any tactic that generates link clicks but damages trust or violates community rules.
