# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Event organizers in LATAM (weddings, quinceañeras, corporate events). Small business owners juggling clients, quotes, inventory, payments and event-day logistics. Desktop is primary (web), mobile (iOS/Android) secondary for on-the-go checks and quick captures. They want control, professionalism, and less chaos — many are migrating from WhatsApp + spreadsheets.

## Product Purpose

Solennix is a cross-platform event-management suite (iOS · Android · Web · Go backend): events, clients, products/recipes, inventory, quotes, contracts, payments, staff and checklists in one place. Success = an organizer runs their whole operation here and looks more professional to their clients (portals, quotes, contracts).

## Brand Personality

Elegant · professional · trustworthy — warm but composed. Reference feel: Honeybook, Dubsado. UI copy in Spanish; warm without being casual.

## Anti-references

- Cyan/purple gradient SaaS aesthetics
- Decorative glassmorphism (system Liquid Glass on the iOS navigation layer is fine; glass as content decoration is not)
- Generic icon+heading+text card grids
- Cold enterprise/2015-CRM sterility
- Generic gray dark mode (dark = rich navy, not neutral gray)

## Design Principles

1. Warmth with precision — generous spacing, gold (#C4A265) used with intention, never sprayed.
2. Professional without sterile — confidence and composure, not corporate chill.
3. Information hierarchy first — complex data (finances, schedules, stock) must be immediately scannable.
4. Light and dark of equal quality — dark mode is navy-rich (#1B2A4A anchor), never an afterthought.
5. Typography as brand — clear scale, decisive weight contrast.
6. Platform-native always — HIG on iOS (toolbar actions, no FABs), Material You on Android, web conventions on web. Same UX capabilities, native expression each.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Dynamic Type / font scaling respected on all platforms (no fixed text sizes).
- VoiceOver/TalkBack: composed labels on compound cards, labels on all interactive elements.
- Reduce Motion honored for all non-essential animation.
- Never color as the only state indicator (status badges carry text).
- Minimum 44pt/48dp tap targets.
